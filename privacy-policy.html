Project Desk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Project Desk is a project management app for Windows that runs entirely on your own computer. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does — and does not do — with it.

Summary

  • Project Desk does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.
  • There is no account, sign-in, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • The app does not connect to the internet. Everything you enter stays on your PC.

Information you enter

You may enter names, email addresses and phone numbers of people, clients and subcontractors, along with project details, costs and invoices. This information is stored only in a local database on your computer (in the app's private data folder) and in backup files saved to a folder you choose (by default, your Documents folder). It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

How the app works internally

Project Desk runs a small local engine on your PC that only accepts connections from the app's own window (address 127.0.0.1, protected by an access key generated each time the app starts). It is not reachable from other computers or from the internet.

Backups and exports

The app makes a daily backup and lets you export your data to CSV or JSON files. These files are saved only where you choose. If you choose a folder synchronised by a cloud service (for example OneDrive), that service's own privacy policy applies to those copies.

Deleting your data

You can delete any record inside the app. Uninstalling Project Desk removes its local database; backup and export files you saved elsewhere remain until you delete them.

Children

Project Desk is a business productivity tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
